# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a respectful, harassment-free community for everyone, regardless of experience, identity, background, or language.

Be constructive, assume good intent, focus criticism on the work, and respect differing language varieties and levels of AWS experience. Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language, publication of private information, and promotion of leaked exam content are not accepted.

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when necessary to protect the community. Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through the contact method on their GitHub profile.
